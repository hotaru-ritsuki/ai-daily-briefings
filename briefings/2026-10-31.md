  - 🐛 `sonnet`/`opus`/`haiku` aliases on Vertex and Bedrock fall back to an older model mid-session instead of erroring.

## 8. 🎯 One thing to try today (≤30 min)
**Run subagents at lower effort than your main session.**
1. `claude update`, confirm `claude --version` is 2.1.316.
2. Start with: `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_EFFORT=low claude` (value names per the effort levels you already use; check `/help` if `low` is rejected).
3. Give a task that fans out, e.g. "Use three subagents to summarise each package under src/app/, then give me a combined list of NgRx effects without error handling."
4. Rerun without the variable and compare wall time and `/cost`.
Success: subagent results are equally usable at noticeably lower cost/time. Also grep your settings and scripts for Opus 4.8 fast-mode pins: `grep -rn "opus-4-8\|4.8" ~/.claude .claude`.
