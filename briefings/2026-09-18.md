## 6. 📰 Articles & essays worth reading

### 6a. AI workflows & patterns

- **[Judging Code](https://registerspill.thorstenball.com/p/judging-code)** (Thorsten Ball, Register Spill) — applies Taleb's "naive interventionism" to review: finding *a* flaw is always available and rewarded, so nits become the verdict while end-to-end-tested features go unweighed. His fix: test against your mental model (happy path, edges, prod-shaped data), then scale review effort to blast radius. The most useful lens for reviewing agent PRs today. *~9 min.*
- **[Astra for Coding: Why Are We Doing This Again?](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/9/7/astra-why/)** (Armin Ronacher) — after burning ~4B tokens over 35h letting GPT-6 Astra run a hands-off "software factory" (79 commits, 75k LOC, no usable result), he argues unsupervised agent loops trend toward *neijuan* — ever more effort, no better output. The counterweight to "just add more agents"; guardrails worth copying. *~12 min.*
- **[Context Engineering: A Practical Guide for AI Agents](https://sourcegraph.com/blog/context-engineering)** (Sourcegraph) — the four-pillar framing (write/select/compress/isolate) plus building context *pipelines* for long-running agents; pairs with the 2026 rule of thumb that AGENTS.md over ~150 lines gets "lost in the middle." Practical baseline for scoping subagent context. *~11 min.*
- **[Engineering practices that make coding agents work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owmJyKVu5f8)** (Simon Willison, Pragmatic Summit talk) — the practices that separate agents that ship from agents that thrash: tight feedback loops, tests as the contract, small scoped tasks. Good companion to Ball above. *~30 min (video).*

### 6b. General AI engineering reading

- **[Judging Naive Interventionism at scale](https://dev.to/jamilxt/i-ran-the-numbers-on-armin-ronachers-1200-agent-run-every-bad-habit-in-it-exists-in-my-2jog)** (DEV) — runs the actual token math on Ronacher's $1,200 experiment and maps each failure to a habit most of us share (no acceptance criteria, no kill-switch, unbounded loops). Sharper as a checklist than as commentary. *~8 min.*
