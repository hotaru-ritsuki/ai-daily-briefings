## 8. 🎯 One thing to try today (≤30 min)

**Audit your hooks against 2.1.300's new `updatedInput` validation.**
1. `claude update` (confirm `claude --version` ≥ 2.1.300).
2. `grep -rn "updatedInput" .claude/ ~/.claude/` to find hooks that rewrite tool input.
3. Trigger each once; any hook emitting extra keys will now have them silently dropped, and invalid input denies the call — fix the JSON shape to match the tool's schema.
4. Also `grep -rn "remote-control" scripts/ .github/` for `--sandbox` flags and replace with `sandbox.enabled` in settings.
