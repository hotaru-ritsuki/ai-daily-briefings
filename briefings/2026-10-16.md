## 🎯 8. One thing to try today (≤30 min)

**Cap your unattended runs' usage-limit waits (CC 2.1.301).**
1. `claude --version` (confirm ≥ 2.1.301; update if not).
2. In the environment/settings for your scheduled routines, set `CLAUDE_CODE_RETRY_WATCHDOG_MAX_USAGE_LIMIT_WAIT_MS=900000` (15 min).
3. Run one routine and confirm it fails fast rather than sleeping when limited; then decide whether the failure should notify you. 
Bonus: enable the new OTel `tool_decision` classifier attributes if you export telemetry.
