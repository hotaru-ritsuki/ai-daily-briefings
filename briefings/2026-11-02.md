## 8. 🎯 One thing to try today (≤30 min)
**Check your repo for Opus 4.8 fast-mode dependencies, then test `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_EFFORT`.**
1. `grep -rniE "opus-?4[.-]?8|fast" ~/.claude .claude CLAUDE.md 2>/dev/null` — find pinned models or fast-mode flags.
2. Update to Opus 5.5 where found.
3. Run a multi-subagent task twice: `claude` normally, then `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_EFFORT=low claude`. Compare wall time and quality on a mechanical task (e.g. generating Handlebars template tests).
Success: you know which subagents tolerate low effort and have no Nov 30 surprise.
