## 🎯 8. One thing to try today (≤30 min)

**Run a deterministic fan-out of read-only reviewers with fork subagents, then diff their findings.**
1. `claude --version` (need ≥ 2.1.302 for fork subagents in `-p`).
2. In an Angular/Spring repo: `claude -p "Spawn 3 forked subagents, each reviewing the last 5 commits independently: (a) correctness, (b) NgRx/RxJS subscription leaks, (c) Spring/Kafka error handling. Report only findings that at least two agree on, plus the disagreements." --permission-mode plan`
3. Compare against your own reading. The codecentric note about shared-context agents inheriting the same misreading is the thing to test: give one agent a different brief and see whether it catches what the others miss.
4. If useful, save as `.claude/commands/triple-review.md`.
