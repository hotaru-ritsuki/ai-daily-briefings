## 6. 📰 Articles & essays worth reading

### 6a. AI workflows & patterns
- **[Context Engineering: A Practical Guide for AI Agents](https://sourcegraph.com/blog/context-engineering)** (Sourcegraph). The clearest current synthesis of the "single orchestrator owns continuous context; ephemeral read-only subagents return 1–2k-token summaries" pattern that the field converged on. ~12 min.
- **[Subagent-Driven Development](https://jonathansblog.co.uk/the-subagent-driven-development-claude-code-skill-orchestrate-multiple-agents-like-a-team)** (Jonathan). First-hand CC skill write-up; best line: *the orchestrator is the integration layer — don't ask subagents to integrate their own work or you get duplication and conflicts.* Pairs with `agent-skills`. ~8 min.
- **[Spec-Driven Development with Claude Code in Action](https://alexop.dev/posts/spec-driven-development-claude-code-in-action/)** (alexop.dev). Hands-on account of the spec→plan→implement loop on a real project — concrete enough to copy the structure. ~10 min.
- **[Claude Code diffs need review packets, not trust](https://simonwillison.net/)** (T. De Vos, Jul 16 — mid-July, but on point). Argues AI diffs should ship with *review evidence* (what was run, what changed, what was verified) rather than being trusted; the practical counter to "fail-plausible." ~7 min.
- **[Code Review in the Age of AI](https://addyo.substack.com/p/code-review-in-the-age-of-ai)** (Addy Osmani). The reviewing-discipline companion to his skills pack — where human review still earns its keep when the author is a model. ~9 min.
