## 🎯 8. One thing to try today (≤30 min)

**Check your routines against the 2.1.302 duplicate-rerun fix and measure your MCP context cost.**
1. `claude --version`; if < 2.1.302, update.
2. Start a session with your usual MCP servers and run `/context`. Note how much is consumed by MCP tool schemas before you type anything (the arXiv paper above saw ~40%).
3. Disable the biggest offender (`/mcp`) or try [context-mode](https://github.com/mksglu/context-mode) for the noisiest one, then re-run `/context` and compare.
4. Audit any scheduled routine that posts or commits: confirm it's idempotent, since pre-2.1.302 a partial failure could re-run it from the start.
